Hi 👋 I'm Andrei, a Senior Full-Stack Engineer based in Timișoara, Romania. I view software engineering as a cohesive, end-to-end discipline. With over 14 years of development experience in the web ecosystem, my core focus is building full-stack applications where frontend rendering performance, UI responsiveness, accessibility, and backend architecture operate in perfect synchronization. I also deeply value collaboration, and I genuinely enjoy working alongside others to build great things and improve together.

My architectural philosophy relies heavily on strict typing (TypeScript), modular component design (React, Vue.js, Node.js), and real-time data delivery. Whether I am implementing SSE for live dashboard metrics, optimizing WebSockets for instant server communication, or managing complex state for large-scale enterprise portals, my goal is always to minimize latency and maximize system resource efficiency. I build user interfaces that are not only performant but inherently inclusive, prioritizing semantic HTML, deep accessibility, and flawless UI responsiveness across all devices.

Quality and automation are the foundations of my development lifecycle. I implement strict, multi-layered testing strategies using tools like Vitest, Playwright, and ESLint to ensure system resilience before a single user touches the application. I am also deeply invested in optimizing developer workflows, actively integrating local AI agents and custom automation scripts to streamline processes like commits and code auditing.

Ultimately, I am driven by the mechanics of the machine. Whether I am optimizing VRAM allocation for local AI inference or rendering massive, paginated data sets in the browser, I build software that is robust, scalable, and engineered for reality. When I step away from the keyboard, you can find me staying active through sports or perfecting the hydration of a whole-wheat pizza dough.

Currently, I am focused on my personal projects and open to new full-stack opportunities and technical collaborations. If you're interested in working together or just want to say hi, feel free to reach out!
